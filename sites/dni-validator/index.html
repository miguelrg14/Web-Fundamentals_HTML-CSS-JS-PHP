DNI letter calculator

Type the numbers of your DNI!

Type the 8 digits of a DNI to get its letter, for example 12345678.

Add the letter at the end to check a complete DNI: 12345678Z.

NIEs for foreign residents work too: X1234567 or X1234567L.
