Find the letter of any DNI

Type the 8 digits of a DNI, or a NIE for foreign residents, to get its check letter. Add the letter at the end to check that a complete ID is right.

How the letter works

The number is divided by 23 and the remainder picks the letter from this table. For a NIE, the X, Y or Z at the start counts as 0, 1 or 2.

12345678 mod 23 = 14 → Z

    Examples
    • 12345678 gives the letter of a DNI.
    • 12345678Z checks a complete DNI.
    • X1234567 or X1234567L work the same for a NIE.
    • Spaces, dots and dashes are ignored: 12.345.678-Z.
